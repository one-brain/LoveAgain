## Problem Statement

Young urban professionals and women in metropolitan tech hubs like Bengaluru (specifically Koramangala and Indiranagar) experience acute social isolation and hesitation when attending cultural workshops, indie films, and sporting activities. 
- While disposable income is high (₹1.5L+/month), they lack established local friend networks.
- Dating apps (Tinder, Bumble BFF) collapse into awkward romantic or sexual boundary violations.
- Ad-hoc social hiring on Reddit or Instagram leads to advance UPI scams, unvetted strangers, and zero physical safety guarantees.

Cue solves this by offering **Strictly Platonic, Verified Companion Outings** anchored around specific public activities, backed by DigiLocker government ID checks, automatic check-in timers, and a two-step Indian Rupee escrow payment guarantee.

---

## Demand Evidence

- **Observed Market Transactions**: Real, documented instances in Bengaluru of individuals hiring passionate private chefs to curate homestyle Sunday family lunches and paying out-of-pocket for engaging food/culture experiences rather than generic commercial restaurant deliveries.
- **Organic Workarounds**: Community members in Koramangala already turn to housing society forums, r/bangalore, and informal Telegram channels attempting to rent platonic companions for events, food walks, and badminton sparring, absorbing high friction and awkwardness due to the lack of a verified platform.

## Constraints

1. **Strictly Platonic Charter**: Zero tolerance for romantic or sexual solicitation. All bookings require designated public venues.
2. **Regulatory & Compliance**: 18+ age verification strictly enforced; DigiLocker Aadhaar/Passport KYC required for all companions.
3. **Financial Protection**: 100% of funds held in platform escrow until completion; full refund guarantee on companion cancellations.

## Premises

1. **Activity Anchoring over Generic Friendship**: Cue must position and market strictly as *Curated Activity & Passion Co-Pilots* (not "rent a friend" or "platonic dates") to eliminate boundary creep, avoid Meta/Google ad bans, and attract high-intent users.
2. **Escrow as the Anti-Leakage Shield**: Users will not disintermediate to WhatsApp because platform escrow protects both client and companion against fraud, late cancellations, and unsafe disputes.
3. **Hyper-Local Density**: Achieving network liquidity requires clustering the first 20 companions within a single high-trust 5 km perimeter (Koramangala/Indiranagar).

## Engineering Review (Architecture, Failure Modes & Edge Cases)

### 1. Zero Silent Failures
- If slot availability changes between request creation and checkout, `BookingService` returns `HTTP 409 Conflict` with a human-readable error (`"This activity slot was just reserved by another member. Please choose another time window."`).
- Frontend modal catches error, avoids crashing, and redirects user back to slot selection.

### 2. Double-Click & Re-entrancy Protection
- In `BookingRequestModal` and `BookingCheckoutModal`, enforce strict mutation state disables (`isSubmitting = true`) to prevent duplicate Razorpay order creation or double booking submissions.

### 3. Escrow State Invariants
- `PaymentStatus` strictly transitions: `Pending` -> `HeldInEscrow` -> `ReleasedToCompanion` (or `RefundedToUser`).
- Payout to companion UPI is only released when either:
  1. Both client and companion confirm meetup conclusion.
  2. 24 hours elapse post-meetup with zero dispute or boundary reports filed.

### 4. Database Schema Impact
- **Zero Schema Migrations**: The Koramangala Activity Drops wedge stores verified venue names and activity categories in existing JSON requirements columns and companion specialty lists.
